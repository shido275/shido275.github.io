Biography

Artist Story

X/i\D is an artist and producer building a world around sharp production, cinematic visuals, and direct emotional writing. Hailing from Auckland, New Zealand, they have spent years honing a diverse style that moves seamlessly between driving synthetic drums, atmospheric samples, and vocal versatility.

Blending trap textures, synthwave aesthetics, and raw lyricism, their catalog represents a relentless pursuit of forward-thinking alternative rap. Working independently under FAKE Entertainment, they deliver complete sonic experiences—handling their own engineering, production, visual layout, and web systems.
